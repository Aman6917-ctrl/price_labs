# Frequently Asked Questions

## Pricing product

### Does PriceLabs set my prices automatically?
Only if **auto-sync / auto-push** is enabled for that listing and channel. Otherwise you review recommendations and push manually.

### Will PriceLabs lower my prices without limit?
No. Min price (and seasonal min overrides) bound the floor. If recommendations always hit the minimum, revisit base price or market positioning.

### Can I use PriceLabs with Guesty / Hostaway / Lodgify?
Yes — via PMS integrations. Connection steps vary by PMS; see the integration guide for your PMS. Channel-direct Airbnb/Booking.com sync is also available.

## Accounts

### Can multiple teammates access one account?
Yes. Invite users under **Settings → Team** with roles: Admin, Manager, Analyst (read-only).

### How do I transfer listings between accounts?
Export is not a full move. Contact support with both account emails and listing IDs. Self-serve transfer is on the roadmap.

## Billing

### When am I charged?
Subscriptions renew monthly or annually based on plan. Listing count tiers update at the next renewal unless you add listings mid-cycle (prorated on some plans).

### What happens if I exceed listing limits?
New listing imports are blocked until you upgrade or archive unused listings. Existing sync continues.

## Data

### How often is market data refreshed?
Core market metrics refresh daily. Event signals update as new market bookings appear.

### Can I export prices?
Yes — calendar CSV export and API `GET /prices`.
